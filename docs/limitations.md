# Limitations

Conditions under which this projection should not be relied upon, and known
weaknesses in its construction.

---

## L-001. Not a reproduction of supervisory models

This projection is an independent challenger estimate. The Federal Reserve's
supervisory models are not publicly disclosed. Agreement with published results
would indicate that the approach taken here arrives at a similar answer, not
that the supervisory models have been replicated.

---

## L-002. Segments without usable loss history

Two segments cannot be estimated from their own history.

Loans to nondepository financial institutions represent approximately 9 percent
of total loans and were not separately reported for most of the estimation
sample. See A-009.

Purchased credit deteriorated loans and loans acquired in the People's United
transaction carry acquisition-date fair value marks that affect reported
charge-off behavior in the quarters following the acquisition.

---

## L-003. Scope exclusions

The following components of the supervisory stress test are not modeled.

| Component | Reason |
|---|---|
| Global market shock | M&T reports zero standardized market risk-weighted assets and is not subject to this component |
| Largest counterparty default | Applies to firms with substantial trading, processing, or custodial operations |
| Operational risk losses | Not separately modeled; treated within the noninterest expense projection |

The first two exclusions are properties of the institution rather than
simplifications. The third is a simplification.
